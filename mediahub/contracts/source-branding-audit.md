# Source branding audit criteria

A source-level MEDIA•HUB migration is complete only when a clean Android build, without branding-copy scripts, resolves all user-visible product identity from MEDIA•HUB resources/components.

Audit launcher label, adaptive/legacy icons, TV banner, splash, onboarding, wordmark composable, account/QR, Home/navigation, settings/about/support copy, notifications/toasts, locale overrides and any BuildConfig app/service names.

Nuvio may remain in copyright/license attribution where legally required and in unchanged internal package names during gradual namespace migration. It must not remain as the presented product identity.
